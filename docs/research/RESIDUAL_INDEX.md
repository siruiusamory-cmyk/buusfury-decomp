# The residual index: every raw row, its verdict and the census that carries it

Generated by `tools/research/residual_index.py` (`python tools/research/residual_index.py`).
One line per `docs/research/RAW_REMAINDER.csv` row, in the class its census document gives it.
`--check` re-derives this file, refuses a row with no verdict line or with a verdict line in
two census documents, and asserts the totals against the gate. One row per class and region,
and the document that carries it.

**The raw-data total is 3128 rows / 494250 B == the gate's `gate.incbin_ranges()` 494250 B. The
identity copies are 1024 B == `gate.union_ranges(gate.identity_ranges())` and are not raw rows.**

## The verdict classes

| verdict class | rows | bytes | what states it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `irreducible by measured reason` | 2008 | 157282 | the row's census line names the measured instrument that reached it and the null that instrument leaves: no field, no count, no extent |
| `pending-image-route` | 49 | 2828 | a `graphics` row or the map block's 24 B carve-out `0x29B308`: the image route ran and does not carry it, because the row holds no frame, or its frame crosses a carrier token, or the route refused it, or it is the carve-out |
| `others found` | 1071 | 334140 | the row carries a measured citation (`code:<ROM address>`, a decoded reader, or `runtime`, a read observed at run time) and still states no extent |
| `identity` | 0 | 1024 | not a raw row: a copied interval the image already holds, on its own line in the gate's accounting |
| **total** | **3128** | **494250** | the row sum is the gate's raw-data total; identity is counted apart |

The `pending-image-route` class is every `graphics` row of the census and the map block's 24 B carve-out
`0x29B308`. 11 of its 49 rows are map block rows of `R02` and `R12`-`R15` that no ten-region census
carries, and 38 are the ten regions' `graphics` rows that `docs/research/REGION_READ_CENSUS.md`
counts. The rows the image route leaves are measured in `docs/research/MAP_BLOCK_LAYOUTS.md`
and `docs/research/REGION_READ_CENSUS.md`, and the carve-out's own re-encode in
`docs/research/ROUTE_TO_100.md`.

## The join

`--check` walks every row of the census against every verdict table of every document above.

| what | rows | bytes |
|---|---|---|
| rows with no verdict line | 0 | 0 |
| rows with a verdict line in two documents | 0 | 0 |
| rows whose own document carries two sections on them | 1513 | 105541 |

A document may carry more than one section on a row - the null and the read verdict are two
measurements, and the second names the readers the first bounds - and that is not a second
verdict. Two documents on one row is, because neither is then the row's census.
