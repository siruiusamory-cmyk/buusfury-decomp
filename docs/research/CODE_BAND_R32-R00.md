## What the method entry is worth

`text_tables.anchor_failures` resolves the three methods TEXT-TABLES-001 pins (0x04AAF4 slot 0 and slot 8, 0x04C484 slot 0x68) before any share is believed. 0 method targets land in the population's rows; 0 of their walks end in a return (`BX`, `POP {..,pc}`, `MOV pc`, `LDR pc`), against 0 of the same number of random even addresses drawn from the same rows with the same walk (seed 20261017). What the target adds over the control is the evidence that a class-table slot names a function, and every body is still proved byte-exact by the converter before it is emitted.

## What the `PUSH {..,lr}` shape is worth

The shape is a halfword `0xB5xx` at an even offset whose walk ends in a return: 16 row(s) and 51 site(s) in this population. The control is the same scan over a byte-permuted copy of each row (seed 20261017): 18 of the 51 real sites end in a return against 4 of the 66 sites the permutation produces. The shape is only accepted where a body also reassembles byte-exact through the converter, and a row a pool citation names keeps that class: a named consumer outranks a coincidence of shape.

## The remainders, with their reasons

- `pool-data` rows have a named consumer (the loader site that holds their address as a pool
  word) but they are the pool, not the code: a standalone `DCD`/`DCB` dump of the retail bytes
  is the raw-data class, not a route (`docs/research/ROUTE_TO_100.md`), and no tracked data
  family re-derives these bytes from fields, so they stay INCBIN.
- `unreached` rows carry no entry and no pool citation at this denominator. They stay INCBIN
  with their region as their evidence, exactly as the region table states.
- a `method-target` row whose walk yields no instruction, or whose body the assembler refuses,
  is a remainder too; the converter's census carries its own reason verbatim.
