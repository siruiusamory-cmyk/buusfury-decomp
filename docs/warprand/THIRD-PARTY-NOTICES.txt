KNOWN GAP - OWNER DECISION REQUIRED
-----------------------------------

Full licence TEXTS for the LGPL-licensed components (Qt, PySide6, shiboken6) are
NOT bundled, and since the release archive now contains only the executable and a
README, not even this notice reaches the person who downloads it. Redistributing
an LGPL library is normally expected to carry a copy of the LGPL text and a
written offer of the corresponding source, and nothing here substitutes for
either.

This gap is recorded rather than papered over. The application is currently a
private, unlisted build. If it is to be distributed publicly, either add the
notice and the Qt/PySide6 licence texts back into
`scripts/package-warprand.ps1`'s staged files, or link Qt statically and comply
with the LGPL that way.
